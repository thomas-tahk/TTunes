A. The Set

A red bakelite tabletop radio: the video is the glowing screen, the queue is stations along the tuning dial, requests arrive as paper slips.

Fits because you "turn on" a radio and walk away. One big knob does Play; everything else is furniture you glance at. Owner controls live on the back of the set.

B. Afterglow

The song title fills the whole window in color pulled from its thumbnail; one yellow drawer holds the queue, requests and owner tools.

Fits a second monitor or a background tab: readable from across the room, nothing to manage. The page changes color with every song, so it feels alive without asking for attention.

C. The Log

Fits a few friends: the fun is seeing who sent what to whom. The music plays; the thread is the reason to check back.
