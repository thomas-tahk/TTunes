## How to try it
Once #28 is done I deploy and post the URL on [#33](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/33). Then:
1. On your phone (not signed in), open the URL, press **Play**. Under the player: **Request a song**. Type a name and a dedication, paste a YouTube music link, **Send request**.
2. It shows in the **Requests** feed as "Coming up" and plays after the current song, on every screen, with no action from you.
3. On your computer, signed in: request a non-music video from the phone. Your panel shows a badge; tap **Deny**.
4. In your panel's request settings pick **Off**. Reload the phone: the box is gone, the feed stays.

Watch for:
- The feed under the player: name, cover, title, dedication, a status chip.
- Your panel: the waiting-count badge, **Approve / Deny / Remove / Keep**, and the Open / Approve / Off setting.
- **Keep** on a played request adds it to your library.

**Fixed along the way** (whole-milestone review + QA):
- Keeping a request put it at the front of the shuffle, so it replayed a song or two later; it now counts as heard this round.
- You could line up a song a listener request already had lined up (it would play twice): refused with "That song is already coming up as a request." Undo after a delete no longer re-queues such a song either, and says so.
- Remove on a lined-up request now says "Removed. It will not play." (the old note, "Removed from the feed.", undersold it), based on the request's real state at the moment you tapped.
- Everything that holds YouTube details (requests, trash, old activity titles) is now cleared at 28 days, because Vercel's free daily job can run up to an hour late.
- The *open* setting's hint was wrong ("queued right away"); fixed.
- Commits: [8d4b270](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/8d4b270), [fc9a442](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/fc9a442), [84d16a3](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/84d16a3), [fb62783](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/fb62783), [32fd3e3](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/32fd3e3).

**Known, not fixed:**
- The feed shows at most 30, and fewer once old requests are cleared at 28 days.
- A listener tab that never pressed Play sees *Off* only after a reload (keeps the free database asleep). A tab with its own request waiting keeps checking.
- The "Sending…" state isn't announced to screen readers; after you tap Deny, keyboard focus lands on the row (its buttons are gone).

**Stuck:** none.

## Decisions that need you (most important first)
1. **Wording I made up.** Say if any should read differently:
   - Request box: "Request a song", "Your name", "Dedication (optional)", "Song link", "Send request", hint in approve-first mode "Every request waits for approval."
   - After sending: "Your request, *song*, is queued and will play soon." / "…is waiting for approval." + "You can send another once it has played."
   - Feed chips: "Coming up", "Playing now", "Played", "Waiting for approval", "Expired", "Requests off"; empty feed "No requests yet. Be the first."
   - Refusals, e.g. "You already have a request waiting or lined up. Wait for it to play.", "That's 5 requests this hour. Try again later.", "That song is over 15 minutes, which is too long for requests."
   - Your side: setting hints ("Music under 10 minutes plays without waiting; anything else waits for you."), notes "Approved. It is queued.", "Denied. It will not play.", "Removed. It will not play.", "Removed from the feed."
2. **Removing the request that's playing right now doesn't skip it.** It leaves the feed, the song finishes. Skip is still its own button. Say if Remove should also skip.
3. **A queued (auto-approved) request has Remove, not Deny.** One tap still stops it. Say if you want the button called Deny there too.
4. **Merge order:** M2 ([#37](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/pull/37)) first; then I open this milestone's PR into main.
