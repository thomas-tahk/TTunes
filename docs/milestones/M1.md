# Milestone 1: On my phone, from the cloud

**Done-gate:** "I open TTunes on my phone and music is playing within a second, from the cloud." Read as **one tap, then music within a second**, because phone browsers won't start sound without a tap.

**Status:** passes with everything running in the cloud sandbox, i.e. the real production Docker image talking to Postgres and an S3 store (MinIO standing in for R2). It is **not deployed yet** and you haven't tried it: the live cloud needs your Cloudflare R2, Neon and Fly.io accounts (steps below, about 20 minutes).

- **Cold open on a phone:** a QA agent ran the real production image (512 MB memory, like the Fly machine) and a Chromium iPhone 13 profile with a saved login. Time from the tap to sound:
  - 130–150 ms on localhost
  - 330–400 ms on a simulated LTE link (100 ms latency, 10 Mbps)
  - 0.9–1.2 s on a slow link (300 ms, 1.5 Mbps)

  In every run the phone started on the station's track, within 0.1 s of the station's position.
- **Resume across devices:** desktop played a track to 0:31; the phone opened, showed "Tap to pick up at 0:31", and one tap played it from 31.75 s while the desktop was at 31.78 s.
- **Track to track:** 7–11 ms from the end of one track to the next one playing. The next track was fetched 8–12 s early, and the browser cache served it.
- **Survives a server restart:** the container was killed in the middle of a track. The phone kept playing from its buffer, came back in sync, and the queue was intact.
- **Owner password:** a stranger guessing 10 times locks that stranger out for 15 minutes; a phone that is already logged in keeps playing.
- **Tests at the head:** 211 server tests (against real Postgres and MinIO, none skipped) and 33 browser checks at phone and desktop size, all passing. GitHub Actions runs all of it on every push, plus a Docker build and smoke test, and is green.
- Screenshots: [`m1-shots/`](m1-shots/) (phone `*-390x844.png`, desktop `*-1280x800.png`).

**Scorecard so far:** [`docs/chief/scorecard.md`](../chief/scorecard.md). First-pass reviews 3/7, merged/planned 7/7 (stuck 0). Escaped bugs and guesses kept get filled in after you play it.

## How to try it

1. **Put it in the cloud.** Follow [`docs/deploy.md`](../deploy.md) top to bottom. In short:
   - Make an R2 bucket and an API token scoped to it, and copy the account ID, key ID and secret.
   - Make a Neon project and copy its connection string, ending it in `?sslmode=verify-full`.
   - `fly launch --no-deploy --copy-config`, then one `fly secrets set …` command with your password, a session secret and those values, then `fly deploy`.
2. **On your phone:** open `https://<your-app-name>.fly.dev`, type your password once, add it to the home screen, and drop a few songs in with **+ Add music**.
3. Close it. Open it again, tap the big orange button, and music starts where the station is.
4. Open it on your laptop too: it picks up the same track at the same spot.

Watch for:
- **First open:** a big "On air now · Tap to pick up at m:ss" card at the top. After the first tap the bar at the bottom is the player (seek, play/pause, next), and your lock screen shows the track with play, pause and next buttons.
- **Up next:** the next 6 tracks you queued, or the shuffle pick. "+ Queue" on any song; "Queue full" when there are 6.
- **A song the phone can't play** (for example an old codec on an older iPhone) shows "Couldn't play X — skipping." and moves on. Files that aren't audio are refused at upload with the reason shown.
- **iPhone Safari** is the one thing nobody here could test (only Chrome's iPhone emulation). If anything misbehaves, it's most likely there.

**Fixed along the way** (found by reviewers or the QA run, before you saw anything):
- Postgres: a dropped idle database connection crashed the server, and reordering the queue failed. Both fixed in [#5](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/5).
- Password: behind Fly's proxy, one stranger could have locked you out of logging in, and a malformed login could write your password into the logs. Both fixed in [#9](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/9).
- Station: a track could be skipped when two devices reported its end, and a late report could rewind the station. Fixed in [#7](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/7).
- Startup: a mistyped database host made the server hang silently. It now stops within 10 s with one line ([merge d7a4d71](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/d7a4d71)).
- From the QA run:
  - Non-audio files were accepted, and one could stop the station forever. Uploads are now checked ([merge 15534a4](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/commit/15534a4)) and the player skips a track it can't play.
  - Songs with accented or Japanese titles in WAV tags failed to upload.
  - "Play now" right after opening started the new song at the old song's position.
  - Failed uploads showed no message.
  - An old paused tab could drag the station backwards.
  - The player bar clipped the time on long artist names.

**Known, not fixed:**
- Pressing Next while offline gets undone when the phone reconnects, because the server never heard it. The phone then rejoins the station.
- A song deleted on one device stays in another device's list until that device reloads.
- Some VBR MP3s without a length header report the wrong length. The playing phone still moves on at the real end; only an unattended station waits longer.
- A deliberately malformed WAV can crash the server through a bug in the tag-reading library. Only you can upload, and Fly restarts the machine.
- An idle station picks its next shuffle track only when someone opens it. That's fine for one listener.

**Stuck:** none.

## Decisions that need you (most important first)
1. **Create the three accounts and deploy** (R2, Neon, Fly.io; [`docs/deploy.md`](../deploy.md)). I picked Fly.io over Railway: about $3–4 a month for one always-on 512 MB machine, against Railway's $5 floor plus usage ([research](../chief/research/host-pricing.md)). The image runs anywhere, so switching hosts or moving to the home lab is a config change. Say if you'd rather use Railway or Render.
2. **Lockout rule.** 10 wrong passwords from one device locks that device out for 15 minutes, and that includes your right password. Logged-in devices are never affected. The alternative is slowing down answers instead of locking out.
3. **One tap to start.** Phone browsers forbid sound without a tap, so opening shows a big play card, not instant sound. The alternative is a full-screen "tap anywhere" overlay. Same tap, bigger target.
4. **Devices follow the station, they don't fight it.** If another device skips, a playing phone finishes its last few seconds and then joins. A deliberate play-now on another device switches immediately. A phone that can't play a song while another device is playing it only shows "Can't play X on this device" and doesn't skip it for everyone.
5. **Wording on screen.** "On air now", "Tap to pick up at 0:31", "Up next", "Queue full", "Couldn't play X — skipping.", "Your station is empty. Add a few songs and it starts shuffling." Change any you don't like.
6. **The library starts empty in the cloud.** The songs you uploaded on your Mac (`data/tracks.json`, `uploads/`) aren't copied over. Re-drop the folder on the phone or laptop, or I write a one-time import.
7. **Merge.** The PR goes from `m1-cloud-phone` into `main`. You merge it; I can't push to main.

Every other interpretation is in [`docs/questions/M1.md`](../questions/M1.md).
