## How to try it
Merge this PR (it deploys to the live site in about a minute). Then:
1. Open https://t-tunes-navy.vercel.app in Chrome (not Brave: Brave was the buffering) and press **Play**. Pause for a bit, then resume: it carries on from where you paused. Drag the YouTube bar back: it stays there.
2. Top right, **Look**: switch between **Afterglow** and **The Set**. Reload: your pick stays.
3. Sign in with the footer link **Owner sign-in (station owner only)**. Open **Owner tools**, search a song by name, tap a result (it expands), then **Play next**. It shows as Up next.
4. On your phone (not signed in), tap **Request a song**. Type a name, paste a YouTube music link, **Look up**, then **Send request**. It plays after the current song. This is also the M3 live check ([#33](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/33)).

Watch for:
- The two looks: Afterglow (big poster type, colours pulled from the song's cover) and The Set (stage poster, warm tones).
- The new wording: the "Request a song" tab, "Anyone can request a song." under Up next, the footer sign-in link, and the one-time hint "Got a song? Request it here."

**Fixed along the way:**
- **Playback:** a song cued while paused started by itself. YouTube's own pause during a song swap was mistaken for yours. A slow-loading song started seconds behind live.
- **Layout:** the player was squeezed off 16:9 with the drawer open. The look switch showed selected and unselected backwards in The Set. Disabled buttons were hard to read. A long title pushed the player off the first screen.
- **Add flow:** two cards could be open at once; errors appeared off-screen; focus was lost; Escape misbehaved.
- **Whole review:** the preview errors now say "YouTube unavailable" when YouTube is down. A listener on a shared office network is no longer told *they* did too many link checks. The README says the station is live. The portfolio image script works with the new layout.

**Known, not fixed:**
- Pressing pause while a song is still buffering isn't noticed; it starts anyway. Follow-up: [#44](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/44).
- On a 320px-wide phone the player is 320×200, slightly wider than 16:9, so the video shows thin bars. The 200px minimum wins there.
- The Set on a landscape phone: only the header and player fit on the first screen; scroll for the rest.

**Stuck:** none.
