# Milestone 5: layout & polish

**Done-gate:** "In Chrome I press Play, open Settings and switch Look, Layout and Panel while it keeps playing. The player stays visible and the indicator says On air. On my phone, a listener request for an age-restricted video is refused with a clear message."

**Status:** passes locally (dev server, local Postgres, real YouTube player in headless Chrome, YouTube search/lookup stubbed). The production build passes too. **Not live yet.** Merging this PR deploys it to https://t-tunes-navy.vercel.app.

- **Gate run (QA):**
  - **Switching while playing:** 9 switches while playing (2 looks, 2 layouts, 5 panels). The player kept going every time, with no jump back and no reload, and the indicator stayed "On air". A half-typed request survived every switch. All three choices survive a reload.
  - **Phones:** at 375×812 and 320×568 the player was fully on the first screen with the menu open and closed. No sideways scroll.
  - **Age filter:** a listener pasting an age-restricted link sees "That video is age-restricted, so it can't be requested." You (owner) can still add it. Listener search uses YouTube's strict SafeSearch.
  - **Indicator:** Paused shows "Paused". A song change goes On air → Buffering → On air.
- **Player checks:** across every look × layout × panel × listener/owner at 7 sizes, the player was never covered, 16:9, at least 200px tall, and had no sideways scroll (216/216 combinations).
- **Tests at the head:**
  - Typecheck clean.
  - 289 unit/API tests pass (1 skipped, the old pooled-database test).
  - The production build passes.
  - 15 browser checks pass: settings, stacked, layout, indicator, add by link, your controls, moderation, requests, sync, Afterglow, The Set, playback, paused owner, unplayable song, slow start.

## How to try it
Merge this PR (it deploys in about a minute). Then:
1. Open https://t-tunes-navy.vercel.app in Chrome and press **Play** (top right of the player on a laptop).
2. Click **Settings** (top right; just a gear on phones). Switch **Look**, **Layout** (Side / Stacked) and, in Afterglow, **Panel** (Dark tinted, Neutral black, Frosted, Cream, Yellow). The song keeps playing. Reload: your picks stay.
3. Under the player: the calm indicator says **On air**, Paused, Buffering or Between songs. There are no fake bars.
4. On your phone (not signed in), tap **Request a song** and paste a link to an age-restricted YouTube video. It's refused.

**Fixed along the way:**
- **Production build:** the build failed on an invalid panel style. The dev server hid it, so it was caught only in the final review. Builders must now run the real build.
- **Settings menu:** the panels leaked desktop edges onto phones. On phones Settings is a single gear, so the player sits higher.
- **Layout switch:** switching layout lost typed text and moved keyboard focus. Escape closed a hidden drawer.
- **Content filter:** a hung YouTube check could stall the station. It now times out after 2s and the song plays.
- **Cleanup:** about 30 duplicated size rules moved into one shared file, with no visual change. The READMEs now cover all of this.

**Known, not fixed:**
- **Age filter limits:**
  - It trusts YouTube's age-restricted flag.
  - If the check fails or times out, the song plays.
  - Explicit lyrics aren't detectable.
  - A request can show as Up next and then be skipped if it's age-restricted.
  - The real YouTube field was checked against Google's docs, not a live age-restricted video. The first real one you see will confirm it.
- **Phone held sideways, Settings open:** the player scrolls below the fold. The alternative was a pop-over covering the player. The same applies to you (owner) on a 320px phone in Afterglow.
- **Up next on a 1280 laptop:** it sits just at the bottom edge.
- **Portfolio image:** [`docs/preview/ttunes-preview.png`](../preview/ttunes-preview.png) still shows the M4 yellow panel.
- **Pause during buffering ([#44](https://github.com/thomas-tahk/TTunes/issues/44)):** closed. The fix broke fast double-skips, and the original bug isn't reachable by a real tap.

**Stuck:** none.
